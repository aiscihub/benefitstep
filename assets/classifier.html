What this model has—and has not—been tested on

Three broad classes. Utility bills, rent records, county requests and benefits receipts are not separately trained categories. “Other document” covers the comparison examples we trained on; it does not prove a document is irrelevant to an application.

The held-out pilot had 163 documents: 117 pay statements, 13 invoices/receipts, and 33 other documents. The classifier returned 126 correct suggestions and abstained on 37 at the selected threshold. These results do not establish real-world benefits-document accuracy or a reliable unknown-document detection rate.

Scores are uncalibrated model outputs, not accuracy, eligibility or authenticity probabilities. PAYSLIPS and CORD contain publisher annotations; the selected FieldBench comparison records are synthetic. Dataset/source differences may make the benchmark easier than actual applicant documents.
