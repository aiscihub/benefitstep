# BenefitStep 0.3.6 — local Chrome Web Store upload candidate

Prepared October 6, 2026 from commit 4e7f6a8 plus the changes committed with this release. This package has not been uploaded or published.

- **More of the CalFresh form is filled from documents.** A confirmed child care invoice fills the care record (child, provider, amount paid). A rent receipt fills the rent row of question 11, and a utility bill fills the row its service lines name. An award letter fills the unearned income record. A medical statement fills the medical expense record when a household list shows the patient is 60 or older. A monthly pay statement fills that job's gross for the month, and its labelled lines fill the employer phone, hourly rate and weekly hours. The Yes box above a table is ticked when a record below it is filled; nothing is ever answered No. The CalFresh form map now has 157 boxes, up from 75.
- **People details from a household list.** A document with a "Household members" heading and one line per person, each with a date of birth, is read as a list of people. Those names are offered for the household, and a ticked person's birth date goes on both forms. The relationship goes on the CalFresh roster when the list marks the owner as self or head of household. From the owner's own list, a mailing address and other names used go on the CalFresh form, and the mailing address and email go on the Medi-Cal form.
- **Pictures and scans get more than one try.** A picture has no text reader to fall back on. A reading by on-device AI that has not finished after 40 seconds is stopped and started once more. A reading that does not identify the document, lacks the amount that kind of document is read for, or gives no date is done a second time, and the second supplies only what the first lacks. If the address or the kind of service is still missing, the model is asked for those alone. An address given as one line is split into street, city, state and ZIP.
- **"How often" is filled only from what a document shows.** It is filled as monthly when the document says so or its period is one month long. When a reader answers "unknown", that word is not written in the box.
- **What your documents filled.** Each application editor lists every added document with the sections it filled, or the reason it filled none: details paused in Review, not confirmed yet, or no section for that kind of document.
- **Summary of filled content.** A finished draft opens with "What was filled": each form section with its PDF page, one line per record with the values, and the document or entry each line came from. A page number jumps to that page in the preview. "What is left for you" groups the open answers by section. The same summary shows before generating and on the Application page, and the download package includes it as `what-was-filled.txt`.
- **Text in short boxes.** Text in the forms' short one-line boxes was drawn too low, and viewers cut off the foot of each character. It is now centred on the letters.
- **Which copy is loaded.** The footer of the side panel shows the extension name and version.
- **Demo.** `demo/earley-household/` now holds eleven fictional documents for a five-person household, in two packs: `pdfs/` has all eleven as PDFs and needs no AI; `documents/` has three of them as pictures, which only on-device AI can read. Either pack fills 72 boxes on six pages of the CalFresh form and 61 boxes on the Medi-Cal form. With on-device AI off, the picture pack fills 58 CalFresh boxes.

Limits: the newly mapped CalFresh boxes are draft mappings like the rest, and the pixel comparison of printed positions has not been run for them. On-device AI answers vary between runs and can be wrong. The demo pictures are clean renderings of PDFs; phone photos and skewed scans were not tried. Image-derived values need visual confirmation. The app has no switch to turn on-device AI off once Chrome has it ready. Every detail still needs confirmation against its source. The name split, birth dates and relationships are proposals to check. Signatures, consent choices, Social Security numbers and the eligibility questions are left to the applicant. Numeric dates are read month first. Interface text added since 0.3.4 is English only; Spanish and Chinese show it untranslated.

Validation: the 133 Node checks in `tests/` passed. In headless desktop Chrome 154, the offline Alex, household, Spanish and Chinese checks passed (four Alex PDFs, 57 confirmed facts, zero evidence-check findings). The Earley household demo was run on the unzipped upload archive with each pack: `pdfs/` with on-device AI off, and `documents/` with it on, where the three pictures were read by on-device AI. Both runs ended with the CalFresh draft at 72 fields and the Medi-Cal draft at 61, with no page errors. Not run: a visible Chrome window or an everyday Chrome profile, phone photos, image decoding with the earlier sample images, and store screenshots. Results are in `package-validation.json` and `release-browser-check.json`.

The ZIP retains existing store branding, icons, CSP and sidePanel-only permissions. Development preview routes and renderer fixtures are excluded. Official PDFs remain unsigned drafts; independent linguistic and PDF-mapping review remains incomplete.
