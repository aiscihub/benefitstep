BenefitStep policy inventory and first-screen audit

BS-POL-AUDIT-001 | Version 0.1 | October 4, 2026

1. Decision and scope

The first screen can be a short program-routing and initial income-reference check. It cannot certify all CalFresh or Medi-Cal eligibility requirements. Keep the accepted four-step layout; revise the policy meaning of inputs and outputs rather than building another full application.

This is an initial inventory of policy families with a focused audit of the visible CalFresh panel. It is not an exhaustive, independently approved legal rules engine. The Medi-Cal panel, dropdown options, implementation code and result screens were not supplied for this review. Their behavior is unknown. All implementation instructions below are proposals; no existing package was modified.

Important correction: the earlier blanket idea that Medi-Cal never requires residency proof is not safe to encode. DHCS ACWDL 26-15, dated September 9, 2026, supersedes 26-04 and 26-04E and describes electronic and administrative verification. Do not infer a mandatory upfront bill upload from that correction. [MC-S02]

The displayed income number is correct: $5,500/month for four people matches the current 200% MCE/BBCE reference, effective October 1, 2026 through September 30, 2027. It is not a universal CalFresh eligibility cutoff. [CF-S04, CF-S05]

3. Answer to the residency example

CalFresh

California residence is a baseline condition. There is no minimum time the household must already have lived there and no requirement for a permanent dwelling or fixed mailing address. Presence solely for vacation is different. [CF-S01]

Residency generally needs verification, with unusual-case exceptions when it cannot reasonably be accomplished. Existing rent, mortgage, utility or identity evidence should be reused where possible. Reasonable documentary evidence or collateral contact can establish residence; a particular document type cannot be made the sole permitted method. [CF-S02, 273.2(f)(1)(vi), (f)(4)]

Medi-Cal

Federal residence rules have category-specific branches, including adult intention/work-entry circumstances and temporary absences. Living in the state and a mailing address are not interchangeable. The current California verification instructions are in ACWDL 26-15. [MC-S01, MC-S02]

The county attempts electronic verification. Under stated conditions verification can be derived from the primary applicant for co-resident members without conflicting information. When electronic routes do not resolve residence, administrative verification is requested; accelerated enrollment can apply while verification is completed. BenefitStep has no access to those government verification systems. [MC-S02]

For the letter's stated category of an individual age 21 or older who can indicate intent and is not institutionalized, one recent California rent/mortgage receipt or utility bill in that person's name is a listed method. Other documents and specific declaration alternatives exist. Do not generalize this list to every child, institutionalized person or household member. [MC-S02, PDF page 4]

Implementation result

Yes + matching record should not set eligible=true. Store a self-reported fact and a separate evidence relationship. A potential record supports only its relevant person, period and factor. County verification remains unknown unless independently supported by a scoped, user-confirmed official record.

Never turn an arbitrary file date into a legal 30-day rule. Keep "recent" source-dependent and a county request's actual period explicit. An old California address, another person's name, temporary absence or different service/mailing addresses may require clarification; they are not automatic fraud findings. User confirmation concerns the extracted facts, not authenticity.

Residency wording

Keep: Do you live in California?

Offer Yes / No / Not sure. Helper: You do not need a permanent address. Provide a targeted help path for temporary absence, a recent move, a visit, or applying for someone else, rather than asking everyone for move dates or a lease. This answer is a shared starting fact only; program-specific and individual circumstances still need to be resolved when relevant. [CF-01, MC-01]

Household wording

Proposed: Including you, how many people live together and usually buy and prepare food together?

Expanded help should clarify that spouses living together and children under 22 living with a parent normally must be grouped even when food is separate. Other mandatory combinations and exceptions belong in the detailed roster logic, not an overloaded first-screen sentence. The original helper omits the parent relationship for the under-22 condition. [CF-S03]

Keep an explicit Not sure answer. A count is only an estimate until the roster, applying/nonapplying members and special household rules are resolved. Do not deduce meal sharing from a document address.

Income wording and result

Keep the estimate/range; label the specific reference. Suggested helper:

For 4 people, the starting income reference is $5,500/month. Some households need a different calculation.

Source details should identify 200% MCE/BBCE, the period, and the unassessed conditions. The exact $5,500 boundary belongs to the at-or-below band. A range crossing a threshold produces an unresolved comparison, not a forced pass/fail. Gross wages, net pay, year-to-date values and self-employment receipts are not interchangeable. [CF-S04; detailed treatment queued under CF-05]

For the selected "More than $5,500" answer, the most this screen supports is:

Above this starting income reference. We need more details before deciding which checks apply.

The app must not display "Not qualified." For example, county guidance explicitly describes further evaluation for some elderly/disabled households above the MCE limit. [CF-S05]

Immigration wording

Remove Could one applicant meet immigration rules? as a required legal self-assessment on this limited initial screen. It asks the user for a legal conclusion instead of an observable fact. Keep citizenship/immigration unassessed, not implicitly satisfied.

When the later application branch needs it, identify who is applying and ask only the necessary program-specific factual questions, with Not sure / enter directly with county routes. The September 30, 2026 CDSS errata reiterates that immigration verification must not be requested for a nonapplicant; financial treatment of nonapplicants is a separate matter. [CF-S07, CF-S08]

Medi-Cal must not inherit the CalFresh answer. ACWDL 26-13 differentiates federal funding changes from state-funded full-scope coverage for affected groups; the new/existing-enrollment rules require a separate current branch. [MC-S06, MC-S07]

Program selection and status

Keep the selected benefits, but remove any implication that completing CalFresh questions checked Medi-Cal. A tab can show CalFresh: starting check completed / Medi-Cal: not checked. Residency may be reused as a proposed shared fact; household and financial conclusions cannot. A benefit-selection switch is product navigation, not another policy requirement.

5. Minimum-input contract

For the limited first CalFresh check, use three questions after program selection: residence, estimated food-household size, and income range. All have an uncertainty/skip route. Replace "Start with four quick questions" with "A few quick questions to choose your next step."

This is minimal only because the result is explicitly limited. A full eligibility opinion would need additional facts. Do not market these three answers as satisfying all baseline rules.

Show additional questions only when the next supported decision actually depends on them. Examples: age/disability pathway before dismissing above-reference income; income type when business receipts are involved; a detailed household roster when the size is uncertain; county when a waiver or local handoff requires it. These branches remain subject to detailed rule approval.

Before any Medi-Cal income result, identify the applicant's supported category and household methodology. Child, adult, pregnancy-related and non-MAGI pathways are not one income test. Age bands can be a minimal routing input; exact date of birth and diagnosis should not be collected solely for initial routing. [MC-S03, MC-S04, MC-S05]

Do not add full address, SSN, immigration identifier, photo ID, employer details, rent amount or medical history to the first screen merely because later processing may need them. Ask at the point of use, or let the user enter sensitive identifiers directly on the official site.

7. Source governance and dates

Store each rule's authority, source section, jurisdiction, program, person/category scope, issued date, effective interval, supersession, required facts, verification alternatives, unknown behavior and reviewer approvals. Store requested proof separately from the eligibility predicate.

A current primary state instruction may supersede an older instruction. A recently crawled public webpage may still contain old thresholds. Do not select policy by domain or retrieval date alone. Governing law, amendments, implemented state options, active waivers and applicable judicial orders must be reconciled before release. Conflicts become an explicit blocked-rule review task; they are not resolved by a language model.

Important current anchors: CalFresh I-40-26 (October 2026 reference period); CalFresh ACL 25-92 plus I-29-26E (applicant-specific noncitizen review); Medi-Cal 26-15 (residency verification); Medi-Cal 26-01 and enclosures (category-specific FPL); Medi-Cal 26-13 and current changes guidance (funding/scope/enrollment). Future-dated provisions must not run early.

This pass does not certify every waiver, legal challenge, student exception, financial exclusion or special-category rule. Those remain explicit inventory work rather than silently assumed coverage.

8. Implementation invariants

  1. A screening answer is unverified self-report. A file is candidate evidence. User confirmation is not government verification.
  2. Each result names the exact check performed and the conditions not assessed.
  3. Unknown never becomes false, zero or ineligible by default.
  4. Keep applicant, household, program, period and policy-version scope separate.
  5. No generic must-upload requirement; an actual county request creates a scoped outstanding task.
  6. Preserve official filing access while withholding unsupported prepared answers or conclusions.
  7. Keep prior submitted snapshots immutable. Newly added evidence is not automatically more current.
  8. A document can support more than one fact without proving every fact or every member's eligibility.
  9. Do not activate a new policy from scraped prose. Require domain review, implementation review and tests.
  10. This audit does not validate the screenshot's privacy statements or native processing. Those require separate installed-extension testing.

9. Test plan and audit validation

screen_1_test_plan.json contains 26 acceptance scenarios. They are not run; no app source or installed behavior was tested. The build script validates unique identifiers, source links, disabled policy flags, date-bounded reference metadata and not-run test states only.

Before release, assign domain and engineering reviewers, resolve the queued rule families, test the actual dropdown and result logic, and obtain the Medi-Cal panel for a separate visible-screen audit. Do not interpret this report as a complete legal or security certification.
