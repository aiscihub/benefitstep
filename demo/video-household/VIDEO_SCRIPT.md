# BenefitStep video — 90-second household story

## Before recording

Open an empty session, choose Local text reader in Processing options, and prepare the main-packet folder. Open index.html in a separate local tab for source close-ups. Read extracted/RESULTS.md to know the actual suggestions. Record at a readable browser width; use a PDF close-up when showing small figures. Keep the small fictional-data marks visible.

| Time | Screen action | Suggested wording |
|---|---|---|
| 0–12 s | Show the household gallery, then the two pay statements and utility bill. | “Jordan has earnings records, rent paperwork and bills from several places. BenefitStep helps bring those records into one preparation flow.” |
| 12–25 s | Choose main-packet once. Let actual processing finish. | “Add the available files together. A local model suggests broad types, and the information reader prepares details for review.” |
| 25–40 s | Open a pay source; compare gross pay with its net deposit. | “The earnings amount and the bank deposit are different. The source stays beside the prepared information.” |
| 40–55 s | Show the utility source: current 140, previous 800, total 940. | “This bill includes older unpaid amounts. The current service charge is $140; $940 is the total due.” |
| 55–70 s | In Confirm details, deliberately change Current utility charges from 140 to 940. Show Doctor, then restore 140. | “If I accidentally use the total due, Package Doctor compares it with the source and asks me to check the amount.” |
| 70–82 s | Point to an Unknown suggestion or unsupported bank record; optionally show the separate scan. | “The helper can leave information unresolved. It does not turn a missing answer into zero or claim every document is understood.” |
| 82–90 s | Show the reviewed details and prepared package. | “I review the available facts and choose what to use in the official application. Preparation is not submission.” |

The utility mistake is a deliberate user correction for the demonstration. Do not imply the extractor made that mistake automatically. Use the actual on-screen methods and predictions.
