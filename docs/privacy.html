Privacy

Your documents stay on your computer

BenefitStep reads your pay stubs, bills and receipts on your own computer and fills in a draft of the official forms. You check every value, then sign and submit the application yourself.

  • No account
  • Nothing uploaded
  • Open source
Read on your own device

Your documents are read inside the extension. They are not sent to BenefitStep, to a cloud AI service or to any agency.

No account, no tracking

There is no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics.

Kept only if you download it

Your work is cleared when you close the panel. Download your package to keep a copy.

On-device AI is your choice

Photos and scans can be read by the AI built into Chrome, which runs on your computer. It stays off until you turn it on.

What BenefitStep does not do

It is not a government service. BenefitStep is independent of BenefitsCal, the state and county offices.

It does not decide whether you qualify. Only your county can do that.

It does not submit anything. You sign and submit your application through BenefitsCal or your county.

It can misread a document. Check every value against the original before you use a draft.

BenefitStep Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026. Applies to BenefitStep 0.3.2.

Information you choose to use

BenefitStep processes the answers you enter and documents you select. These may contain names, addresses, dates of birth, household details, financial information, health-related information and other document contents. We use this information only to provide the local preparation features you request: initial checks, document recognition, evidence review and unsigned application drafts.

Local processing

The extension does not send your documents or answers to the developer, cloud AI services or an agency. Libraries, the document classifier and PDF resources are packaged locally. When available, Chrome's built-in AI processes document contents on your device. Chrome may separately download its browser-managed model. The extension does not include advertising or analytics, sell personal data, or use your data to train a developer model.

Temporary work and downloads

Working documents and answers are kept in temporary memory, not saved as extension history or synchronized through a developer account. Clear my work removes this working state. Reloading or ending the extension page's lifetime also clears it. Downloaded PDF or ZIP packages are files you keep outside the extension; closing the extension does not delete them. You control their storage, sharing and deletion. Downloaded packages are not password-encrypted.

Websites and sharing

BenefitStep does not read your BenefitsCal page, browse your history or submit an application. If you follow an external official-site link, that site operates under its own privacy policy. Documents are shared with an agency or another recipient only when you separately choose to send or upload your downloaded files.

Your controls

You can edit or remove details, remove source documents, clear your work, or stop processing. Do not share a downloaded package with anyone you do not intend to receive its contents. Fictional demo data is labeled and is not valid application evidence.

Policy updates and contact

We will update this page when the extension's data practices change. For questions, use the publisher contact or support link displayed on BenefitStep's Chrome Web Store listing.
