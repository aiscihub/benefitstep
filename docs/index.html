How it works

Four steps, at your own pace

  1. 1

    Quick check Optional

    Answer a few questions to see how your income compares with the CalFresh income guide. It is a first look, not a decision.

  2. 2

    Add your documents

    Add the pay stubs, rent receipts, bills and letters you already have, as PDFs or photos. BenefitStep finds the names, dates and amounts.

  3. 3

    Review

    See each detail with the document it came from. Fix what is wrong and confirm what is right.

  4. 4

    Get your drafts

    BenefitStep fills in the official forms with what you confirmed and lists what is still missing. Download the package and finish it yourself.

What BenefitStep does not do

  • It is not a government service. BenefitStep is independent of BenefitsCal, the state and county offices.
  • It does not decide whether you qualify. Only your county can do that.
  • It does not submit anything. You sign and submit your application through BenefitsCal or your county.
  • It can misread a document. Check every value against the original before you use a draft.

In your language

Choose your language in the BenefitStep panel. The translations cover the main steps and have not yet been checked by professional translators. The form questions and the official PDF forms are in English.
